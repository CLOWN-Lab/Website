Who we are

C.L.O.W.N is a very serious AI research team working on Deep Learning Models.

We study how models learn, how to optimize them, and what they can do once they work. We are a team of members from many countries, and we run our research the way we build our models: together, and with a lot of iteration.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need ML experience to join?

No. You can contribute more than you think you can, and you will still learn a lot about ML along the way.

What can I do besides writing code?

A lot, whatever you study or work in. Some examples:

  • Academic research: reading papers, surveying previous work, forming hypotheses and research questions.
  • Data science: finding, designing and preparing data.
  • Software engineering: understanding existing code, architecture, test environments, ML-Ops.
  • Statistics: metrics, evaluation methods, benchmark comparisons.
  • Technical writing: documentation, context and project requirements.
  • Marketing: record keeping and communication about our work.
  • Business and economics: practical uses and cost optimization.
  • Physics and other sciences: domain knowledge for the problem at hand.
How much time do I need to commit?

There is no minimum. Work weekly, monthly or even yearly. We host regular meetings to keep in touch, and you are free to join them or skip them.

Can I join from anywhere?

Yes. The team is international, and anyone can join.

Can I pitch my own project?

Yes. Every project we run is pitched by a member.

Are your projects public?

Ongoing projects are often private. Depending on the team members’ preference, some can be found on GitHub before their official release. Completed projects are published in the project gallery.

Can I use a project on my CV?

Yes, it looks good on a CV. A private project can be made public if you need it for a resume or other career or research needs, and some completed projects might be submitted for academic publication.

How do I get in touch?

Email us at clown.lab@icloud.com or say hi on our GitHub organization.
